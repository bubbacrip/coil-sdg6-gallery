01 / UNDERSTAND

The problem we see.

Polluted water harms nature and people. We looked at how much water we use, how it gets polluted, and who has access to clean water.

OUR CHALLENGE

Water is polluted faster than we notice.

Waste, plastic and chemicals that end up in rivers, lakes and streams make water unsafe for fish, animals and people. Clean drinking water and safe sanitation are not available to everyone, and every one of us can help protect them.

Learn more about SDG 6 ↗

Without water there is no life.

We want to show why clean water matters, how we pollute it, and what each person can do to protect it.

Three actions you can take today

01

Don’t litter

Never leave waste near rivers, lakes or streams. It ends up in the water.

02

Reuse

Use reusable bottles instead of single-use plastic and recycle correctly.

03

Protect the source

Don’t pour chemicals down the drain and join local clean-up actions.
